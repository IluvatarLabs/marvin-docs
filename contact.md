# Contact & Support

Get in touch with our team for help with issues, questions, or feedback. Before reaching out, you may find a quick answer in our [FAQ](faq.md).

## Getting help

Email us at [support@marvinsci.com](mailto:support@marvinsci.com). We respond to all inquiries within 1-2 business days.

## Reporting an issue

To help us resolve your issue quickly, include as much of the following as applicable:

- **What you were trying to do** — a brief description of your goal
- **What happened** — including any error messages or unexpected behavior
- **Your project name** — so we can look up your account
- **Steps to reproduce** — if the issue is repeatable, list the exact steps
- **Browser and OS** — for UI-related issues, include browser version and operating system

The more context you provide, the faster we can help.

## Response expectations

We aim to respond within 1-2 business days for all inquiries.

For urgent issues — such as billing errors, data loss, or service outages — add "URGENT" to your email subject line. We'll prioritize these accordingly.

## Enterprise support

Enterprise customers have access to dedicated support channels. Contact your account manager for details on your specific SLA terms and support options.

## Feature requests

We read every feature request that comes in, and your feedback directly shapes our roadmap. To submit a request, email us at [support@marvinsci.com](mailto:support@marvinsci.com) with a description of what you'd like to see and why it would help your workflow.
