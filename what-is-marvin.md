# What is Marvin?

Marvin is an autonomous research agent that conducts deep, multi-step investigations on your behalf. You give it a question or a topic, and Marvin takes it from there — reading the relevant literature, forming hypotheses, designing and running analyses, and returning structured conclusions you can act on. You can let it run unsupervised, check in whenever you like, or step back in at any point. Think of it as a tireless research collaborator that does the legwork and hands you the findings.

## What Marvin does

Marvin turns an open question into a finished body of research, end to end.

1. **You ask a question or name a topic.** It can be broad ("What's the evidence that X affects Y?") or narrow ("Run this specific analysis and interpret the result").
2. **Marvin investigates.** It reads and synthesizes the relevant literature, pulls together what's already known, and identifies the gaps worth probing.
3. **It forms hypotheses.** Rather than hunting for a predetermined answer, Marvin develops specific, testable hypotheses grounded in what it has read.
4. **It designs and runs analyses.** Marvin plans the work needed to test each hypothesis, carries it out, and interprets the results against the evidence.
5. **It reaches conclusions.** Each finding comes back as a structured result — what was found, how strong the evidence is, and what the open questions are.

You can start a run and walk away. Marvin continues working, and you can monitor progress or jump back in to redirect, refine, or ask follow-ups at any time.

## Persistent memory

Marvin remembers what it has learned — across runs, across questions, and across projects. Each investigation builds on the ones before it.

- **Findings persist.** A conclusion reached in one run is available to the next, so Marvin doesn't re-derive results it has already established.
- **Context accumulates.** When you return with a related question, Marvin brings prior context to bear rather than starting cold.
- **Knowledge compounds.** Over time, Marvin's picture of your research domain gets richer, which makes later investigations faster and better grounded.

This is what turns Marvin from a one-shot tool into a long-term research partner. The more you use it, the more it knows about your work.

## What sets Marvin apart

- **Fully autonomous.** Marvin runs unsupervised. Start an investigation and it works through the full process — reading, hypothesizing, analyzing, concluding — without needing you to drive every step.
- **Persistent memory.** Marvin retains what it learns across sessions and projects, so each run builds on accumulated context rather than starting from scratch.
- **Rigorous, evidence-based process.** Marvin grounds its conclusions in evidence, distinguishes strong findings from weak ones, and is explicit about uncertainty rather than overstating results.
- **Structured results.** You get back organized findings — not a stream of prose — with clear statements of what was found, how confident Marvin is, and what remains open.

## Who it's for

Marvin is built for researchers, scientists, and analysts who need thorough investigation without hand-holding. If you regularly face questions that require digging into literature, reasoning through competing explanations, and backing conclusions with analysis — and you'd rather delegate the heavy lifting than do it manually — Marvin is designed for that workflow.

It's well suited to open-ended questions where the right approach isn't obvious up front, and to follow-up investigations that benefit from remembering what came before.

## What Marvin is (and isn't)

Marvin is a research agent — not a chatbot, and not a substitute for your judgment.

- **Not a chatbot.** Marvin doesn't trade quick messages back and forth. It does sustained, multi-step research and returns structured findings rather than conversational replies.
- **Not a black box that decides for you.** Marvin does the investigation and presents the evidence; you decide what to do with it. It's a collaborator that sharpens your thinking, not a replacement for it.
- **Not a substitute for domain expertise.** Marvin excels at thorough, evidence-grounded investigation. Your expertise still matters for framing the right questions, weighing trade-offs, and making the final call.

Used well, Marvin takes the breadth of reading, analysis, and synthesis off your plate — so you can spend your attention on the decisions that need you.
